Wallos is an open-source, self-hosted subscription tracking tool with a global user base.

## Key Facts

- **Market**: Global (self-hosted)
- **Core capability**: Manual subscription entry and tracking
- **License**: Open source
- **Limitations**: No OCR, no Apple tax detection, requires manual data entry

## Relationship to Subscription Doctor

Wallos validates user demand for subscription tracking but takes a fundamentally different approach -- manual entry vs automated OCR detection. Subscription Doctor's automated pipeline (screenshot upload, OCR, rule matching) solves the friction problem that limits Wallos adoption among non-technical users.
