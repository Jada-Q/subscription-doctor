SubsHub is a Japanese subscription management app that relies on manual entry for tracking subscriptions.

## Key Facts

- **Market**: Japan
- **Core capability**: Manual subscription tracking and management (サブスク管理)
- **Limitations**: No OCR, no AI, no Apple tax detection -- purely manual data entry

## Relationship to Subscription Doctor

SubsHub (along with other サブスク管理 apps) represents the current state of Japanese subscription tools -- functional but friction-heavy due to manual entry. The PROJECT_PLAN positions Subscription Doctor as a leap forward: automated OCR detection replaces manual input, and [[apple-tax-detection]] adds a value layer that SubsHub cannot provide.

SubsHub's privacy posture is relatively strong (manual entry means limited data exposure), but its feature set is weak compared to Subscription Doctor's automated pipeline.
