Trim is a US-based financial tool that scans bank transactions and enables subscription cancellation via SMS.

## Key Facts

- **Market**: United States
- **Core capability**: Transaction scanning, SMS-based cancellation
- **Data access**: Requires bank account connection

## Relationship to Subscription Doctor

Trim is a secondary US competitor that validates the transaction-scanning approach. Like [[rocket-money]], it does not support the Japanese market and requires bank API access, making it unsuitable for privacy-conscious Japanese users.
