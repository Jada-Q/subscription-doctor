Costly is a Japanese iOS subscription management app with privacy-first design — the closest direct competitor to Subscription Doctor's positioning.

## Key Facts

- **Developer**: YUUSUI KASHIWAGI
- **Platform**: iOS only (App Store ID: 6738356072)
- **Privacy**: Data stored on iPhone locally only, no external servers, no account creation
- **Pricing**: Free tier + 月額200円 / 年額1,800円
- **Press**: Covered by 朝日新聞デジタル and 37+ Japanese media outlets
- **Type**: Manual entry (no OCR)
