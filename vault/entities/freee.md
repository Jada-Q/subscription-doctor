freee is a major Japanese cloud accounting platform focused on bookkeeping and tax filing (確定申告).

## Key Facts

- **Market**: Japan
- **Core capability**: Cloud accounting, 確定申告 (tax filing) support
- **Target users**: Freelancers, small businesses, professional accountants
- **Competitive threat level**: Medium -- could enter subscription audit space

## Relationship to Subscription Doctor

freee (along with [[money-forward]]) represents the closest Japanese incumbent threat. However, freee targets professional accounting users, not consumer subscription optimization. The PROJECT_PLAN estimates a 6-12 month window before incumbents like freee might enter the subscription audit niche.

Subscription Doctor explicitly avoids 確定申告 features in V1 to avoid competing directly with freee and to sidestep 税理士法 (tax accountant law) compliance risks.
