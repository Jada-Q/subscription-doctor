Pine AI is a US service that uses an AI phone agent to call customer service lines and cancel subscriptions on behalf of users.

## Key Facts

- **Market**: United States (English-language only)
- **Core capability**: AI-powered phone calls to cancel subscriptions
- **Approach**: Voice AI agent, not screenshot/transaction analysis

## Relationship to Subscription Doctor

Pine AI represents a different approach to the subscription problem -- automated cancellation rather than audit/detection. Subscription Doctor intentionally does **not** offer auto-cancellation (compliance risk in Japan). Pine AI has no screenshot analysis capability and operates only in the English-speaking market.
